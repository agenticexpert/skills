# Brainstorm

1. Read `index.md`: the premise, the audience, `## Notes` and any scenes.
2. Take the angle the ask names:
   - **open:** anything that serves the premise.
   - **focused:** one question, in depth.
   - **audience:** their questions, their problems, what makes them share.
   - **contrarian:** the opposite of the obvious take; a critic's view.

   None named → open on the first brainstorm, else the angle Notes cover least.
3. Generate ideas distinct from each other and from Notes. Name the connections between them and
   the questions they open.
4. Add one block at the end of `## Notes`:

   ```markdown
   ### Brainstorm: <angle>[, <focus>]
   - <idea>

   <connections, open questions>
   ```

5. Write each decision the user makes as a `Decided:` line at the end of `## Notes`, in their words.
   A decision that contradicts an earlier `Decided:` line: leave that line as it is, and end the new
   line `(replaces: <the earlier decision's words>)`. The active decisions are the `Decided:` lines
   no later line replaces.
6. Leave the scene files untouched. Reply with the three strongest ideas, each with what sets it
   apart.

# Resume Evidence — the source-of-truth database

Verified raw material for current and future resumes. Facts live here; wording
lives in the resume. Tailoring searches this, never invents.

## Rules

- No invented companies, roles, metrics, tools, or scale. A guess never
  becomes a fact.
- Unknown → `needs-confirmation`. Weak → `evidence-gap`. Confirmed beats
  impressive.
- Missing PCOPO parts become follow-up questions, not blanks filled by
  plausibility.

## Entry format

```
### <achievement name>
- Company / Role / Period:
- Problem:      (what was limited, broken, slow, risky, manual, expensive)
- Change:       (what moved from before to after)
- Ownership:    (what the candidate personally owned, drove, built, delivered)
- Proof:        (system, scale, users, team size, constraints, before/after)
- Outcome:      (what got better and for whom)
- Tech / Tools:
- Metrics:      (confirmed only; categories to ask about otherwise)
- Constraints:
- Interview defense: (the 30-second spoken version)
- Confidence:   confirmed | needs-confirmation | inferred | risky
```

## Search method — tailoring from the database

Extract the target's needs → match entries → prefer direct over loose,
current over old, specific over broad, defensible over impressive-sounding.

Gap output per target need: matching evidence · strength · missing proof ·
risk if overstated · the question that would confirm it.

## Capture discipline

Capture at the moment of discovery — a strong story surfaced mid-review goes
into the database before it gets compressed into a bullet. Ask only the
questions that materially strengthen the entry.
